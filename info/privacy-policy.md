# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 27, 2026**  
**This document supersedes the revision from July 27, 2025**

This Privacy Policy explains how Erin's Pet Sitting Services ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website, erinspetsittingservices.co.uk (the "Website"), or engage with any of our services. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [erinspetsittingservices@mail.com](mailto:erinspetsittingservices@mail.com).

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 1\. Information We Collect

When you visit our Website or use our services, we may collect the following types of information:

#### 1.1 Automatically Collected Information

We use two analytics tools to collect anonymous data about how visitors use the Website: Google Analytics and Umami. This data may include:

* Your IP address (anonymised);  
* The type of device, browser, and operating system you use;  
* The pages you visit on our Website, the time spent on those pages, and the referring website (if applicable);  
* Approximate location data (e.g., city or region, not your precise address).

This data is collected to help us understand how our Website is used and to improve its functionality.

**Google Analytics** uses cookies to track this information. You can learn more about how Google processes data in its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Umami** is a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect any personally identifiable information. All data collected by Umami is anonymised and aggregated. You can learn more at [umami.is](https://umami.is).

#### 1.2 Information You Provide

When you use our Website or services, you may submit personal information through online forms or directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and signature, to request or receive services or to communicate with us. By submitting a form, you consent to our Privacy Policy, as indicated by the statement at the bottom of the form. Additionally, if you contact us directly via the email address provided ([erinspetsittingservices@mail.com](mailto:erinspetsittingservices@mail.com)) or telephone, any information you include in your correspondence will be handled in accordance with UK data protection laws.

## 2\. How We Use Your Information

The information collected via our analytics tools (Google Analytics and Umami) is used solely for the following purposes:

* To analyse Website traffic and visitor behaviour;  
* To improve the Website's design, content, and user experience;  
* To monitor the effectiveness of our Website.

We use the personal information you provide (name, email address, and signature) to:

* Process your requests for pet sitting services;  
* Send you confirmation emails or other communications related to your request;  
* Contact you regarding your inquiries or bookings.

Signatures are included in documents sent to you as part of any forms you fill out and **are not stored separately**.

We do not use automatically collected data to identify individual visitors or to contact you unless you submit your information through our Website forms or reach out to us directly.

## 3\. Legal Basis for Processing

We process the data collected through Google Analytics and Umami based on our legitimate interests (as permitted under UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Our legitimate interest is to maintain and improve the functionality and performance of the Website for all users. As Umami does not collect personally identifiable information or use cookies, its use presents minimal privacy impact.

We process the personal information you provide (name, email address, etc.) based on the following legal bases under UK GDPR:

* Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): If you provide your information through a form and consent to its use for processing your request or sending communications.  
* Contract (Article 6(1)(b)): If the information is necessary to fulfill a contract for pet sitting services or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or withdraw consent (see Section 8).

## 4\. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Website uses cookies through Google Analytics. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us track usage patterns.

**Google Analytics Cookies:** These cookies collect anonymised data about your visit. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by adjusting your browser settings to block cookies (see Section 5).

**Umami:** Umami does not use cookies or any persistent identifiers. It collects only anonymised, aggregated data and does not track you across sessions or websites.

**Cloudflare Turnstile:** We use Cloudflare Turnstile on our document signing page to verify that form submissions are made by humans rather than automated bots. Turnstile works by analysing browser and device signals without displaying a traditional CAPTCHA puzzle. Cloudflare may process limited technical data (such as your IP address and browser characteristics) as part of this verification. No persistent cookies are set by Turnstile, and the data collected is used solely for security purposes. You can learn more about how Cloudflare processes data in its [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

We do not currently use any other cookies or tracking technologies beyond those described above.

## 5\. Your Choices Regarding Cookies

You can control how cookies are used on your device:

Most web browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings. Note that blocking cookies may affect your experience on the Website.

You can disable Google Analytics tracking specifically by using the opt-out tool linked above.

## 6\. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties, except as follows:

**Google Analytics:** Data collected via Google Analytics is processed by Google in accordance with its own policies. We have configured Google Analytics to anonymise your IP address to enhance your privacy.

**Umami:** Anonymised, aggregated analytics data is processed by Umami. As Umami does not collect personally identifiable information, no personal data is shared through its use.

**Third-Party Cloud Services:** Some information or document generation associated with our services may be processed by third-party cloud service providers. These providers process data on our behalf and in accordance with applicable data protection laws and their own privacy policies.

**Legal Obligations:** We may disclose information if required to do so by law or to comply with a legal process (e.g., a court order).

## 7\. Data Storage and Security

Data collected via Google Analytics is stored on Google's servers, which may be located outside the UK. Google complies with data protection standards, including the UK-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable. Anonymised analytics data collected via Umami is stored on Umami's servers; as no personally identifiable information is collected, this presents minimal privacy impact.

**In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will notify affected individuals and the ICO within 72 hours, as required by law.**

All client information is stored securely and is only accessed by authorised personnel. We retain personal information (name, email address, and signature) for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted.

We take reasonable steps to protect the data we process, but no online system is entirely secure. If you contact us directly via email, your correspondence will be stored securely and accessed only by authorised personnel.

Our services are not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If we become aware that a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it as soon as possible.

## 8\. Your Rights

Under UK data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

* **Access:** You can request a copy of the data we hold about you (e.g., name, email address, or signature). Note that analytics data collected via Google Analytics or Umami is anonymised and cannot be attributed to you individually.  
* **Rectification:** You can ask us to correct inaccurate data.  
* **Erasure:** You can request deletion of your data (though this may not apply to anonymised analytics data).  
* **Restriction:** You can ask us to limit how we use your data.  
* **Objection:** You can object to our use of your data based on legitimate interests.  
* **Portability:** You can request your data in a transferable format (if applicable).  
* **Withdraw Consent:** If you provided consent for us to process your data, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise these rights, please email us at [erinspetsittingservices@mail.com](mailto:erinspetsittingservices@mail.com). We will respond within one month, as required by law. If you're unhappy with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [www.ico.org.uk](https://www.ico.org.uk).

## 9\. Third-Party Links

Our Website may contain links to external sites (e.g., Facebook, Rover). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites.
